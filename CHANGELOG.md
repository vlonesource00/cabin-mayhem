# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **Replaced the airliner fuselage with streamed ship compartments.** The 198 lines of procedural cabin, seat and overhead-bin geometry are gone from `src/three/cabin-world.ts`, and so is `src/three/scenario-loader.ts` and its single-GLB scenario. In their place: `src/data/ship-layout.ts` is a Zod-validated compartment graph, `src/three/compartment-loader.ts` loads one room and enforces its contract (`CM_<ID>_ROOT`, a `CM_PORTAL_<TARGET>` empty per declared portal, the draw-mesh budget) or refuses it, and `src/three/compartment-streamer.ts` keeps the occupied room and its neighbours resident, holds two-hop rooms at reduced detail with their dressing hidden, and evicts everything else as the crew moves. A room that fails to load degrades to a greybox of the same dimensions with the same doorways rather than leaving a hole, and `canvas.dataset.assetMode` still reports `glb` or `fallback`. Four compartments are authored — `atrium`, `cabin-corridor-a`, `bridge`, `engine-room` — each generated deterministically by `tools/blender/compartments/build_compartments.py` and merged by material to 10–13 draw meshes against a budget of 40.
- **Purged the airliner from what the player reads and sees.** The HUD eyebrow is `MS CABIN MAYHEM / DECK LOG`, the restart button is `SAIL ANOTHER SHIFT`, the aeroplane icon is a ship, and the debrief verdicts talk about ports and going overboard instead of airports and airspace. The atrium itself was re-authored: it had been built on a two-aisle-two grid of thirty-two chairs, which is an aircraft cabin regardless of what the room is called. It is now a lit feature column with a spiral stair around it, fore and aft balconies with the middle left open to the full double height, a piano, a bar, and eight angled armchairs standing where the simulation actually seats its guests.
- **Gave the ship real ship handling.** `src/sim/ship-model.ts` is now a rate-command helm: `HelmInput` carries `rudder`/`telegraph` deltas plus `emergencyStop`, and the wheel and telegraph _positions_ live in `VoyageState` and hold where the crew left them. Speed is in knots with an asymmetric response — it builds at `0.09/s` and sheds at `0.032/s`, so there are no brakes at sea, and the crash stop (`B`) slams the telegraph to zero and adds astern thrust at `0.30/s`. Turning radius is emergent rather than authored: rudder authority scales with steerage way, so a dead ship cannot steer at all, sternway reverses the rudder, and the hull heels _outward_ in a turn the way a ship does and an aircraft does not. Heave is differenced twice per tick so the swell is felt as vertical deck acceleration, and `cabinAcceleration` now sums centripetal force with the `g·sin` component of steering trim, heel and both sea-driven hull angles — `src/sim/cabin-simulation.ts` is untouched, so loose props inherit the sea for free. The airliner phase values are gone: `moored` → `preparation` → `departure` → `open-sea` → `approach` → `docked`, with `foundered` as the failure terminal. The HUD's altitude readout became a three-digit bridge heading, and the camera now couples hull pitch and roll.
- **Added the ocean the ship rides on.** One directional-sine wave table in `src/sim/ocean.ts` is the single source of truth: the simulation evaluates it in TypeScript at four hull sample points to derive pitch, roll and heave, and the sea-plane vertex shader is generated from the same table by `oceanWaveGlsl()`, so the water the player sees and the water the hull is fitted to cannot desynchronise. The hull holds the world origin as required — headway is recorded as `sea.drift`, the water sliding beneath the ship, and hull attitude is applied by counter-rotating the ocean. New `SeaState`/`HullMotion` fields on `VoyageState`; the existing `pitch`/`roll` commanded-attitude fields and `cabinAcceleration` are untouched. The cabin now has a visible horizon, so the interior fog thinned from `0.018` to `0.0042`, the background moved from near-black to sea blue, and the camera far plane went from 160 m to 2400 m.
- **Renamed the vehicle vocabulary for the ship.** `FlightState`→`VoyageState`, `FlightPhase`→`VoyagePhase`, `src/sim/flight-model.ts`→`src/sim/ship-model.ts`, `PilotInput`→`HelmInput`, `MissionState.flight`→`voyage`, `PlayerCommand.pilot`→`helm`, and the `flight` mission-event type→`voyage`. Mechanical only: phase values, physics and user-visible copy are untouched, and every check reproduces the previous baseline.

The ship is steered by the crew, moves on a simulated ocean, and is streamed as ~25 discrete compartments across six decks connected by an authored portal graph. Documentation only: no runtime code changed in this entry, and the airliner vertical at `79bb002` is still what runs.
